Take your mortgage with you, or start a new one?

When you move you usually have two routes. Neither is automatically better, and which one suits you depends on your current deal, how much you need to borrow and what else is available.

Take it with you (“porting”)

  • You keep your current rate on the amount you move across
  • Can avoid an early repayment charge
  • You still have to apply, and the lender reassesses you and the new property
  • If you need to borrow more, the extra is usually on a separate product at current rates

Start a new mortgage

  • Compare rates across a wide range of lenders
  • A chance to change your term or how your mortgage is structured
  • May trigger an early repayment charge on your current deal
  • Involves a full application and new checks

Porting is not guaranteed. It depends on your lender agreeing, your circumstances still meeting their criteria, and the new property being suitable security. We’ll check where you stand before you commit to anything.
