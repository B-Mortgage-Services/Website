Moving means juggling a sale, a purchase and your existing mortgage at the same time. We’ll help you work out whether to take your current mortgage with you (known as “porting”) or switch to a new one, what any early repayment charges could mean, and how much you could borrow for your next home.

  • Porting vs. a new mortgage, explained clearly
  • Borrowing more for a bigger home
  • Downsizing and releasing equity

Self-employed or complex income? It’s what we specialise in.

If you’re self-employed, a contractor, a company director or have several income streams, high-street banks can find your income hard to assess. We know which lenders are comfortable with your situation and how to present your figures, whether that’s your latest tax calculations, net profit, salary and dividends, or a daily contract rate.

  • Self-employed and sole traders
  • Contractors (day-rate assessments)
  • Limited company directors
  • Multiple or variable incomes, including bonuses and commission
