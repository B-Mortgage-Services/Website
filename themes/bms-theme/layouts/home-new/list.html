Your mortgage is a commitment. Protection helps you keep it.

Once you have a mortgage, and people who depend on you, it’s worth knowing what would happen if illness, injury or death affected your income.

  • If you die. Could your family carry on living in your home? Life cover
  • If you become seriously ill. Would you have the money to focus on getting better, not on bills? Critical illness cover
  • If you can’t work. How long could your household manage without your normal income? Income protection
